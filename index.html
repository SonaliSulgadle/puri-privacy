Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

🔒 Puri does not collect, store, or share your personal information on any server. All your data lives on your device only.
📱

What data Puri stores

Puri stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your solve history — photos and text queries you have submitted
  • Guides you have saved
  • App preferences (language settings, first launch status)

This data is never transmitted to Puri's servers because Puri has no servers. All storage uses Android's local Room database and DataStore.

When you uninstall Puri, all this data is deleted permanently.

📷

Camera and photos

Puri requests camera permission to allow you to take photos of confusing Korean situations for analysis.

When you capture or select a photo:

  • The image is sent to Google's Gemini AI API for analysis
  • The image is stored locally in your solve history if you keep it
  • The image is never stored on any server operated by Puri

You can clear your solve history at any time from the Profile screen.

🤖

Google Gemini AI

Puri uses Google's Gemini AI service to analyze photos and text queries. When you submit a solve request, your image or text is sent to Google's servers for processing.

Google's data handling is governed by their privacy policy:

policies.google.com/privacy

Google Gemini API terms of service:

ai.google.dev/terms

🔐

Permissions

  • Camera — Used only when you choose to capture a photo for solving. Not used in the background.
  • Internet — Required to connect to Google Gemini AI for photo and text analysis.
  • Vibration — Used for haptic feedback when you take a photo. No data involved.
👤

No account required

Puri does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or log in. There is no user authentication, no user profiles, and no tracking of individual users.

🍪

Cookies and analytics

Puri does not use cookies. Puri does not use any third-party analytics services (no Firebase Analytics, no Crashlytics, no tracking SDKs in V1).

👶

Children's privacy

Puri is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

📝

Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "last updated" date. Significant changes will be noted in the app.
