Puri (풀이) Daily Life Solver for Foreigners in Korea
Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

🔒 Puri does not sell your personal information. Your solve history and saved guides live on your device only and are never uploaded to Puri's servers.
📱

What data Puri stores locally

Puri stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your solve history — photos and text queries you have submitted
  • Guides you have saved
  • App preferences (first launch status, daily usage counters)

This data is never transmitted to Puri's servers. All storage uses Android's local Room database and DataStore.

When you uninstall Puri, all locally stored data is deleted permanently.

📷

Camera and photos

Puri requests camera permission to allow you to photograph confusing Korean situations for AI analysis.

When you capture or select a photo:

  • The image is sent to Google's Gemini AI API for analysis
  • A compressed version is stored locally in your solve history
  • The image is never stored on any server operated by Puri

You can clear your solve history at any time from the Profile screen.

🤖

Google Gemini AI

Puri uses Google's Gemini AI service to analyze photos and answer text questions. When you submit a request, your image or text is sent to Google's servers for processing.

Google's data handling is governed by their privacy policy:
policies.google.com/privacy

Google Gemini API terms of service:
ai.google.dev/terms

📊

Analytics and crash reporting

Puri uses the following Firebase services operated by Google:

  • Firebase Analytics — collects anonymous usage statistics such as which features are used and how often. This data is aggregated and cannot identify individual users. No personal information is included.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — collects crash reports to help identify and fix bugs. Reports include device type, Android version, and the state of the app at the time of the crash. No personal information is included.

These services are governed by Google's privacy policy:
policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of analytics collection by uninstalling the app.

👤

Anonymous authentication

Puri uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication to assign a random identifier to your app installation. This identifier is used to maintain your session and will be used to link your data to an account if you choose to create one in a future version.

This identifier does not include your name, email address, or any personal information. It is not used for tracking or advertising purposes.

🔐

Permissions

  • Camera — Used only when you choose to capture a photo for solving. Never used in the background.
  • Internet — Required to connect to Google Gemini AI for analysis.
  • Vibration — Used for haptic feedback when you take a photo. No data involved.
🍪

Cookies

Puri does not use cookies. The app does not contain any advertising networks or third-party tracking beyond the Firebase services described above.

👶

Children's privacy

Puri is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

🗑️

Deleting your data

All locally stored data (solve history, saved guides) can be deleted at any time from the Profile screen in the app, or by uninstalling Puri.

Anonymous analytics data is aggregated and cannot be individually deleted as it is not linked to any identifiable user.

To request deletion of your anonymous Firebase session, contact us at the email below and include your request. We will process it within 30 days.

If this privacy policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
